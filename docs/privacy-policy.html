Privacy Policy for cURLer

Effective date: August 16, 2026

cURLer ("the app") is developed by hrithikvish ("we", "us", "our"). This page explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices. cURLer is a pocket API client: you paste or build HTTP requests, send them, and review responses.

1. Requests you choose to send

cURLer lets you compose and send HTTP requests — including pasting raw curl commands — to servers you specify. The URLs, headers, and body content you enter are sent directly from your device to the destination(s) you choose. The app does not route, proxy, or inspect this traffic on our behalf, and we have no control over, and are not responsible for, the privacy practices of those third-party servers.

2. Data stored on your device

cURLer keeps a local history of the requests you make (method, URL, headers, body, and response) so you can revisit them later. This history is stored only in the app's local database on your device and is never transmitted to us. It is deleted if you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

3. Backup

The app participates in Android's standard auto-backup of app data to your Google Account, per the OS's default backup rules. You can disable this at any time in your device's system backup settings; doing so does not affect app functionality.

4. Analytics and crash reporting

cURLer uses the following Firebase (Google) services:

  • Firebase Analytics — collects app usage data (screens viewed, feature usage, general device/app info) to help us understand how the app is used.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash and diagnostic reports (stack traces, device model, OS version) to help us find and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config — lets us remotely control certain in-app values (e.g. the About screen). This involves a network call to Google's servers but does not itself collect personal data beyond a Firebase installation identifier.

These services may collect device identifiers, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. cURLer does not show ads and does not use an advertising ID.

5. Permissions

The app requests only the Internet permission, needed to send the HTTP requests you construct and to communicate with the Firebase services above. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your data. We do not share data with third parties beyond Google/Firebase (acting as our data processor, as described above) and the destination servers you yourself choose to send requests to.

7. Children's privacy

cURLer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
